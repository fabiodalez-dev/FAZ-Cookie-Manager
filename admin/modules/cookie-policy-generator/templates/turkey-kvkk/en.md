Personal data obtained through the cookies and similar technologies (pixel tags, gifs, localStorage) on our website — identity, contact, customer transaction, marketing, transaction security and online identifier data — are processed, depending on the cookie type, for the purposes and under the legal grounds set out in Article 5 of the Law:

Legal ground: Article 5(2)(c) — processing is necessary for the establishment or performance of a contract, provided it is directly related to the contract and the personal data of the parties is processed — and Article 5(2)(f) — legitimate interest of the data controller. Your consent is not sought for these cookies and they cannot be refused; disabling them breaks the basic functions of the site.

Legal ground: Article 5(2)(f) (legitimate interest) and Article 5(2)(c) where you have explicitly requested the information society service concerned; your explicit consent (Article 5(1)) where you have not requested it, and for third-party functional cookies.

Legal ground: Article 5(2)(f) for first-party statistical measurement; your explicit consent (Article 5(1)) for third-party analytics cookies and where you have not explicitly requested the service.
- **Marketing / advertising / targeting cookies** — used to present you with personalised product and service promotions based on your viewing history and visitor profile, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns; includes profiling and behavioural advertising. Legal ground: your explicit consent only (Article 5(1)). These cookies are never set without your consent.
